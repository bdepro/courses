Should the Families of Organ Donors Be Compensated?
Name:                                Section:              Date:                     
By the end of this activity, I should be able to:
  1. Explain the economists' proposal to reimburse organ donor families for funeral, hotel, and travel costs.
  2. Distinguish paying for the organ itself (illegal, widely seen as corrupting the gift) from reimbursing a family's incidental costs (the actual proposal on the table).
  3. Evaluate whether this incentive turns an act of sympathy into a transaction, or simply clears a barrier that was blocking sympathy from acting.

Section II  ~20 min
Connecting to Ch. II and Ch. IX — Caring for Others, Worshipping Wealth
Ch. II argues our interest in others' happiness is as natural as self-interest. Ch. IX warns what happens when everything gets treated as tradeable. This proposal sits directly between the two.

Natural interest in others (Ch. II) Genuine concern for a stranger's welfare is to begin with — it doesn't need to be purchased into existence. Does reimbursing funeral costs remove a barrier to sympathy that was already there, or does it substitute payment for sympathy? Defend your answer.
