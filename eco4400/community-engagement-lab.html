Your community engagement consulting project this semester is an economic impact study for Sustainable Alamance, a Burlington, NC nonprofit that runs re-entry programming for people leaving incarceration. The organization’s last analysis, completed in 2017, found a recidivism rate of 9.8% against a 45% state average and a return of roughly $12 per dollar donated — but incarceration costs, wages, and the program itself have all changed since. Your job is to update the numbers, tighten the methodology, and build materials a county commissioner can read in ten minutes and defend in a budget meeting. Wednesday lab sessions are where the work gets done, with structured feedback at each milestone rather than only at the end.
1
Phase 1 — Update the Core Numbers
Recalculate recidivism savings using current incarceration rates and public defender costs, update wage and employment figures from BLS and NC Department of Commerce sources, and rerun the ROI model against actual operational costs. Document every step so a skeptical official can follow the methodology without your help.
SLO 2 — Data collection & ethics SLO 3 — Methods & limitations
2
Phase 2 — IMPLAN Regional Multiplier Analysis
Build county-specific output, earnings, and employment multipliers for Alamance County using IMPLAN, provided to the class free of charge, replacing the 2017 report’s flat 40% national-average multiplier with one grounded in local data.
SLO 3 — Interpret results SLO 5 — Economic modeling
3
Phase 3 — ACS Neighborhood Context
Pull census-tract data on income, employment, and housing from the American Community Survey and map it against program service areas, giving a decision-maker a picture of the neighborhoods Sustainable Alamance serves that a spreadsheet cannot.
SLO 4 — Communicate findings SLO 5 — Visualize data
4
Phase 4 — Public-Facing Deliverables
Turn the analysis into a website, a two-page printed summary for commissioner meetings, and a brand recommendation for the “Second Chance Alamance” identity — all designed so Sustainable Alamance staff can update them without outside help. Your team presents the final work to a live client audience in Week 12.
