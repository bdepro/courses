When Do We Work on Mind?

ECO 1000 — the narrative-writing thread

The written half of Mind & Voice is spread across the semester on purpose. Discovery comes early so the story has time to settle before you draft it, with real feedback and revision time built in before the piece is due. This page tracks just that thread; see the course syllabus for the full weekly schedule, readings, and grading.
