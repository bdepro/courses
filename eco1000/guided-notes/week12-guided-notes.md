## Pitfalls to Avoid

| Pitfall | The error | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| "Government provides it, so it's a public good" | Judging by who supplies a good instead of by its two traits | |
| "People who don't pay must not value it" | Treating free riding as a lack of interest instead of a response to nonexcludability | |
| "The buyer's own gain is the spillover" | Mixing up what the buyer gets with what people outside the deal get | |
| "Transaction costs are the damage itself" | Confusing the cost of the harm with the cost of arranging a deal about it | |
| "A higher price means a better product" | Ignoring that buyers may not see prices or quality before they buy | |
| "One seller means no competition" | Forgetting that easy entry lets potential rivals discipline a lone seller | |
| "Once the rule passes, the problem is solved" | Stopping at the rule's direct effect instead of asking where the money goes next | |
| "Market failure means government will do better" | Comparing a real market to an ideal government instead of to a real one | |
