Section Summary — in your own words before moving on
All Week
Pitfalls to Avoid
Pitfall The error How to avoid it
"The worker who makes the most output gets hired" Using output instead of the revenue the worker adds, compared with the wage
"AI cuts jobs, so it lowers demand for everyone" Forgetting that a tool raises demand for the workers it helps do more
"There's only so much work to go around" Ignoring that savings get spent elsewhere and create new demand
"The economy is fine, so every worker is fine" Treating a claim about the total as a claim about each person
"The tool is cheaper than the wage, so the company switches" Leaving out the time people spend setting up and checking the tool's work
"Everything I pay during college is a cost of college" Counting expenses you'd pay anyway, or money already spent
"Graduates earn more, so college made them earn more" Ignoring a common cause, such as ability and effort
"A signal means the degree is worthless" Forgetting that a signal really does pay the person who sends it
"Worth it to me means worth it for everyone" Mixing up one person's gain with the gain to the whole country
