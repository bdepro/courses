If You Fall Behind

Every safety net in this course, in one place — read this before you panic.

The short version: this course is built so that one missed week, one bad score, or one scheduling conflict does not sink your grade. Each net below is real and already built into the grading — you don't need to ask permission to use one.
Missed or bombed a Puzzle?

You get two attempts on every puzzle — Canvas shows your score between attempts. If you miss the Wednesday 11:59 p.m. deadline entirely, you can still submit for 80% credit until the following Wednesday at 11:59 p.m. And at the end of the semester, I drop your single lowest puzzle score automatically. One bad week does not sink this 15% of your grade.

See the full Puzzles policy
Missed a Check-In, or want a second shot?

Check-In 4 always counts, and if it beats your lowest score from Check-Ins 1, 2, or 3, I replace that score automatically — you don't need to ask. If you miss a Check-In entirely, CI 4 is the makeup; there are no separate makeup exams. CI 4 replaces only one score, so if you miss more than one, the others still count.

Can't make it to class on ? You can take CI 4 instead during the final exam block, — no sign-up or arrangement needed, just show up.

See the full Check-Ins policy
Can't make Viva Week?

Viva Week isn't your only shot. If you can't schedule a slot that week, exam period slots are available, — no approval needed, just use the sign-up link. Pick one or the other; Canvas only lets you reserve a single slot total.

See the full Mind & Voice page
Behind on Guided Notes?

Guided Notes are a true bonus, not a requirement — skipping one costs you nothing and cannot lower your Puzzle score. If you do want the bonus, late submissions within one week still earn 80% of it, matching the Puzzle late policy.
