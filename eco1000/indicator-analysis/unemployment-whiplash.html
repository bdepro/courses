Cyclical vs. frictional unemployment Cyclical unemployment rises with the ; frictional unemployment reflects normal between jobs. A single bad jobs report could be either type. What evidence would help you tell them apart?
Natural rate of unemployment The natural rate is the unemployment level consistent with an economy at GDP, not zero unemployment. How large a jump would convince you the actual rate is now meaningfully above the natural rate?
Business-cycle turning points Recessions are usually confirmed only the turning point, once enough data has accumulated. What is the Sahm Rule's entire purpose, stated in terms of that lag?
