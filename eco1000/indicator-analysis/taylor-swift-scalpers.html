Ticketmaster's presale crashed under demand it called "historic." What does a crash like that tell you about the price the tickets were selling for, relative to what fans were willing to pay? At the face-value price, quantity exceeded quantity .

That gap is a — the textbook signature of a price set below equilibrium.
Resale tickets for some shows reportedly sold for many times face value. What does that resale price reveal that the face-value price hid? The resale price is closer to the true price — what buyers would actually pay if supply met demand.

The gap between face value and resale price is a rough estimate of the left on the table.
A scalper buys a ticket at face value and resells it at whatever the market will bear. In plain terms, what does that scalper actually do? Moves the ticket from someone who got it (maybe by luck) to someone who to pay more for it.
