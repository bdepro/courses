Monday Morning Economist

10% of your course grade · four articles · due Wednesday 11:59 p.m.

A Monday Morning Economist reads the news like an economist — looking past what happened to what drove it. That is the mindset for every annotation. Perusall is an online reading tool built into Canvas. Click the assignment link and the article opens automatically. No separate account needed. Introduced and due dates are listed for each article below. A strong response disagrees with or critiques the article's argument, asks what the author left out, or connects the argument to something from the course. A weak response restates what the article already said.

How It Works

Start Here Quick Start (5 minutes)

Open the assignment through the Canvas link. This is required for credit. Perusall tracks your enrollment through Canvas, and opening it any other way will not register your work.

  1. 1Open the Perusall link in Canvas.
  2. 2Read a section, then highlight a sentence and add a comment.
  3. 3Reply to at least one classmate.
  4. 4Return on a second day for another round.
  5. 5Complete the short quiz.
How To How to Make a Comment

Highlight text by clicking and dragging. Choose Annotate, or use the comment box that appears. Write your thought, question, or connection. Click Post.

To reply, click a classmate's comment and choose Reply. Use Upvote on comments that help you think.

Quality What Makes a Strong Comment

Perusall scores based on how much thinking your comment shows. These four moves earn consistently high scores.

Ask a real question
"Why would demand shift here instead of moving along the curve?"
Explain in your own words
"This is basically saying incentives change behavior because..."
Make a connection
Link the story to supply and demand, inflation, unemployment, trade, or something from class.
Use evidence
Quote or point to a specific line and explain why it matters.

Perusall scores each comment based on how much thinking it shows, and only high-quality earns full credit.

These earn little or no credit
  • "I agree."
  • "This is interesting."
  • Very short posts with no reason, example, or question.
Requirements Credit Checklist

Each newsletter is graded separately, with credit based on what Perusall logs for your session.

  • 1 Post 2 high-quality comments. High-quality earns full credit. Medium-quality earns partial credit.
  • 2 Read to the end and complete the quiz. Scroll through the full newsletter and submit the short quiz at the end.
  • 3 Spend 30–45 minutes actively working. Active means reading, writing, replying, and thinking. Leaving the tab open counts for nothing.
  • 4 Open the assignment 3 times. Visit on at least two different days. Spreading your work improves both your learning and the discussion for everyone.
  • 5 Reply to at least one comment. A question works best. It keeps the conversation going.
  • 6 Receive at least one high-quality reply on your comment. Write a comment good enough that a classmate responds to it. A genuine question or a specific observation invites the best replies.
  • 7 Earn 1 upvote or give 3. Get one upvote on your work, or upvote three classmates whose comments helped you think.
